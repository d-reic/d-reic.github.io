Every few years I promise myself I'll write more in public. This is that promise, version three, now with a site I actually like looking at.

The plan is modest: write when I have something worth writing down, keep it short when it can be short, and never apologise for long gaps between posts.

## What to expect

- **Notes on work.** Things I've learned, half-built ideas, papers I keep coming back to.
- **Reading.** Books and essays that changed my mind about something.
- **Tinkering.** Small tools and experiments, usually with code attached.

> Writing is how I find out what I actually think.

If any of that sounds interesting, there's an [RSS feed](/rss.xml). It's still the best way to follow a small website.
