For a long time I only wrote things down once I understood them. That turns out to be exactly backwards.

The notebook is most useful *before* the idea is clear: when it's still a vague itch, a question that keeps resurfacing, a sentence that doesn't quite work yet. Writing it down gives it somewhere to sit while the rest of my brain catches up.

## Three rules I keep

1. **Date everything.** Future me is a historian with terrible sources.
2. **Never tidy.** A clean notebook is a notebook nobody is using.
3. **Reread on Fridays.** Most entries are noise; a few are the start of something.

This site is, in a sense, the tidy version of that notebook. I'll try not to make it *too* tidy.
